## Treks

### Poon Hill Trek
- Duration: 5 days (from Pokhara)
- Difficulty: Easy — good for beginners and families
- Max altitude: 3,210 m (Poon Hill)
- Best seasons: March–May and October–November
- Highlights: sunrise over Annapurna and Dhaulagiri, rhododendron forests (best in March–April)
- Price: USD 450 per person (group of 2+), USD 550 solo
- Permits needed: ACAP, TIMS

### Annapurna Base Camp (ABC) Trek
- Duration: 10 days (from Pokhara)
- Difficulty: Moderate
- Max altitude: 4,130 m
- Best seasons: March–May and October–November
- Highlights: amphitheatre of peaks at base camp, Jhinu hot springs
- Price: USD 890 per person (group of 2+), USD 1,050 solo
- Permits needed: ACAP, TIMS

### Everest Base Camp (EBC) Trek
- Duration: 14 days (from Kathmandu, including flights to Lukla)
- Difficulty: Challenging — good fitness needed, altitude risk
- Max altitude: 5,364 m (Base Camp), 5,545 m (Kala Patthar)
- Best seasons: March–May and late September–November
- Highlights: Namche Bazaar, Tengboche Monastery, views of Everest
- Price: USD 1,650 per person (group of 2+), USD 1,900 solo, includes Lukla flights
- Permits needed: Sagarmatha National Park, Khumbu Pasang Lhamu Rural Municipality permit

### Langtang Valley Trek
- Duration: 8 days (from Kathmandu)
- Difficulty: Moderate
- Max altitude: 4,984 m (Tserko Ri, optional)
- Best seasons: March–May and October–December
- Highlights: Tamang culture, Kyanjin Gompa, yak cheese factory, close to Kathmandu
- Price: USD 720 per person (group of 2+), USD 850 solo
- Permits needed: Langtang National Park, TIMS

## What's included in every trek
- Licensed guide, porter (1 porter per 2 trekkers)
- Teahouse accommodation and three meals a day on the trail
- All permits listed for that trek
- Ground transport to and from the trailhead

## Not included
- International flights and Nepal visa
- Travel insurance (required — must cover helicopter rescue up to 6,000 m)
- Hotels in Kathmandu/Pokhara, tips, drinks, hot showers and charging on the trail

## Booking and payment
- Deposit: 20% to confirm, balance paid on arrival in Kathmandu

- Group discounts: 10% off for groups of 6 or more

## Frequently asked questions
- **Do I need trekking experience?** Not for Poon Hill. For ABC and Langtang, regular walking fitness is enough. For EBC, prior hiking experience is strongly recommended.
- **What about altitude sickness?** Our itineraries include acclimatisation days. Guides carry first aid and a pulse oximeter. We descend immediately if symptoms are serious.
- **Can I rent gear?** Yes — sleeping bags and down jackets can be rented in Thamel for about USD 1–2 per day each.
- **Is it safe for solo female travellers?** Yes. Female guides can be requested with 2 weeks' notice.
- **Monsoon (June–August)?** We don't recommend these treks in monsoon because of rain, leeches and landslides. Ask us about rain-shadow treks instead.
